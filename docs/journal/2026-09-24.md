## Kho khan / bac hint da dung
- Provider thuoc context nao: luc dau muon gop het (dich vu, gio mo cua, nhan vien, thanh toan) vao 1 context "vi chua mo rong, len microservice thi tach sau" - mau thuan voi lap luan cua chinh minh (Booking khong can sua khi quy trinh nhan su doi). Sau do de xuat gop vao context User "vi deu la nguoi". Bac 1-2 (cau hoi dan dat "ai sua, vi sao sua"; khai niem: bounded context != microservice, cung 1 nguoi co model khac nhau o moi context). Khong mo skeleton.
- Luu gia khi doi gia: nghi toi version gia; bac 2 (khai niem snapshot, so sanh do phu thuoc vao Catalog) -> tu chot luu gia vao booking.
- Ban nhap bounded context: moi bang thanh 1 context, cot "Trach nhiem" chi la "Thong tin X", xoa cac muc Lam/KHONG lam/Can tu context khac. Da chi ra, CHUA sua (de buoi sau).
- Loi cu the trong ban nhap: thieu Payment nhung Booking co trang thai "hoan tien"; username/password nam trong Provider, Customer khong dang nhap duoc; `Calender` thieu ngay; `Booking` thieu `serviceId`; trang thai Booking lech vong doi da chot; `Log` la moi quan tam xuyen suot, khong phai context nghiep vu.

## Rut ra
- Nguoi lam: "Toi thieu hieu biet ve context, dang hieu sai y nghia."
- Bo sung:
  - Bounded context = ranh gioi TRACH NHIEM (1 context chua nhieu bang), khong phai 1 bang. Tieu chi tach: nguoi sua khac, ly do sua khac, vong doi khac - khong phai "deu la nguoi" (gom theo danh tu).
  - Bounded context (logic) != microservice (trien khai). Monolith van co context; ve ranh gioi tu dau thi re, go ra sau thi dat.
  - Tham chieu cheo context bang gia tri ID, khong FK. Du lieu can "dong bang" (gia, gio ket thuc) thi chup lai vao context dung.
  - Diem tot: da luu gia + gio ket thuc trong Booking, chi dung ID, tach danh muc ca khoi phan ca.
