# 2026-09-28 - Chot role o Account, tach Payment khoi Booking

**Phase:** 1 | **Thoi luong:** ~1,5 gio

## Muc tieu buoi
Du dinh 1 tieng: (1) tu viet lai phan Provider/Account + tra loi `role` thuoc dau; (2) lam Payment. Sau 1 tieng chon phuong an B (lam gon Provider/Account, Payment de sau), nhung nguoi lam muon lam tiep khong can gio nen lam luon Payment.

## Da lam
- On tap: "doi so dien thoai vs doi mat khau cung context?" -> nguoi lam tra loi "cung context thay doi thong tin ca nhan" (chua dung). Claude giai thich: mat khau thuoc Account (quy tac bao mat), so dien thoai thuoc Customer (lien lac, Notification) - thay doi doc lap.
- Provider/Account (nguoi lam tu viet, 4 vong review):
  - Them Lam gi / Khong lam / Tham chieu cho ca 2 context.
  - Lo tay xoa phan "Ai sua / vi sao" o vong 2, khoi phuc o vong 3.
  - Chot `role` o **Account**: app khong co chuc nang nao dua tren chuc vu nhan su -> role chi la quyen thao tac. Khach mang role `CUSTOMER` (khong dung null). Ghi danh doi: neu sau nay can chuc vu anh huong nghiep vu thi them cot "chuc vu" o Provider.
  - Dong bo muc Design (chuyen `role` sang Account), chuyen "doi role" sang Account.
  - Provider "Lam gi": tu "luu thong tin nhan vien" -> "nhan vien nay con nhan khach duoc khong", "noi dinh danh 'ai'".
- Payment:
  - Nguoi lam dien bang su kien (tra tien / hoan tien / khong hoan) + tra loi 3 cau hoi.
  - Sau do nguoi lam yeu cau Claude dat cau hoi trac nghiem, nguoi lam chon, **Claude viet phan Payment theo lua chon** (bang su kien day du, ghi chu, ket luan, cap nhat Design + Tong quan). Nguoi lam chua tu dien dat lai phan nay.
  - Ket luan: **tach Payment thanh context rieng**. Booking quyet dinh co hoan / hoan bao nhieu; Payment thuc hien giao dich va giu ket qua (dang xu ly / thanh cong / that bai). Payment phat su kien "da nhan tien" -> Booking chuyen "da xac nhan".

## Kho khan / bac hint da dung
- On tap mat khau vs so dien thoai: gom theo dong tu ("deu la thay doi thong tin"). Claude dua bang so sanh -> bac 3. Khong mo skeleton.
- `role`: lan 1 lap luan mau thuan voi chinh tai lieu ("doi vai tro" dang nam o Provider). Claude dat 2 cau hoi dan dat (thang chuc la quyet dinh nhan su hay bao mat? khach mang role gi?) -> nguoi lam tu dua lap luan dung. Bac 1-2.
- Provider "Lam gi" van la "thong tin X" o vong dau -> Claude goi y cau hoi "Booking hoi Provider dieu gi khi khach dat lich". Bac 2-3.
- Payment - "chuyen tien la nhu the nao?": Claude giai thich thuc hien hoan tien ngoai doi (goi cong, ket qua bat dong bo, khong hoan 2 lan, doi soat). Bac 2 (khai niem).
- Nguoi lam de xuat "dung Kafka/Redis" cho Payment bao Booking -> Claude phan bien: monolith chua co bai toan giua cac process, broker keo theo idempotency/eventual consistency, lo trinh de o Phase 8.
- Nguoi lam de xuat Booking co trang thai "huy kem hoan tien / huy khong hoan tien" (mau thuan voi y dua trang thai hoan sang Payment) -> Claude dua tinh huong cong tu choi hoan.
- Trac nghiem Payment (bac 3-4, dap an nam trong lua chon, Claude viet file). 2 lan doi lua chon sau khi Claude chi ra:
  - "Doi chinh sach huy 1h->2h sua Payment" -> mau thuan voi "Booking quyet dinh so tien hoan" -> doi thanh "sua Booking". Lai la gom theo danh tu ("lien quan tien -> Payment").
  - "Payment goi thang Booking" -> nghe danh doi huong phu thuoc -> doi sang "phat su kien".

## Rut ra
- Nguoi lam: "Viec phan chia theo context qua moi me, toi van bi lan lon giua cac khai niem. Thuong thi toi danh gia no la gi roi thiet ke DB, lam nhu nay kha hay va se chuan DB hon nhung toi chua co kinh nghiem nen bi roi."
- Claude bo sung:
  - Luu y: muc tieu cua bounded context KHONG phai "DB chuan hon" (chuan hoa DB la chuyen khac). Muc tieu la **moi quy tac nghiep vu co dung 1 cho so huu**, de khi nghiep vu doi chi sua 1 cho. DB den sau va di theo ranh gioi do.
  - Cach kiem tra 1 quy tac thuoc dau: quy tac do **can du lieu gi, du lieu do nam o dau**. VD quy tac huy truoc 1 tieng can gio hen + ai huy -> Booking.
  - Tach **quyet dinh** (co hoan khong, bao nhieu) khoi **thuc hien** (tien da ve toi khach chua). Ghi ket qua thuc hien vao cho ra quyet dinh se sai khi thuc hien that bai.
  - Trang thai chi doi qua hanh dong nghiep vu, khong co thao tac "sua status" truc tiep -> nen tang cho aggregate + State pattern o Phase 2.
  - Huong phu thuoc: goi thang thi Payment phai biet Booking; phat su kien thi Payment khong can biet ai nghe. Chon **co che truyen** (in-process / broker) la quyet dinh rieng, de sau.
